One night

There was a night when everything somehow became music.

I was talking to you while working on a song, and the words we'd spoken kept finding their way into it.

Stranger. Attraction. Distraction. Flame.

And somehow the title became Stranger Flame.

Maybe it was just a song. But for me, it became a little piece of that time.

A birthday letter

Happy Birthday, Pavi.

I don't know how many times I've replayed the little moments we've had, but somehow they still feel close.

The bus rides, the random conversations, the ridiculous soup, the walking, the photographs, the late nights, the music — none of them looked like important moments while they were happening.

But sometimes the smallest moments become the ones we remember the most.

I don't want this page to ask anything from you. I don't want it to make you feel uncomfortable. I just wanted to give a little space to some memories that mattered to me.

Whatever life brings you next, I genuinely hope it is kind to you. I hope you find things that make you happy, people who make you feel understood, and places where you can become exactly who you want to be.

And if you ever look back at this chapter someday, I hope you remember it kindly too.

Have a beautiful birthday. ❤️
